### C.36.P:5 - Worked Micro-Examples

#### C.36.P:5.1 - "The Platform Changed The Style"

The editor finds two records. The recommender changed the relative visibility of two dance variants, and the later published clips show a different mix. Those records can support the visibility and publication claims over the clips covered. “The platform changed the style” could also mean that dancers copied a different movement because of the recommendations, or that classes retained the change. Either is a further claim. To establish copying from this source, trace the relevant exposure and the dancers' use of the movement. For causal influence or continued class use, examine the corresponding comparison or later work.

An editorial note for someone deciding how to present the evidence can read as follows.

```text
CulturalEvolutionWordingRecoveryLine:
  triggerSpan: "platform changed the style"
  wordingUse: “platform” names the recommender and its visibility infrastructure
  claimScope: relative visibility and the mix of variants in the covered periods and published clips
  recoveredObjects: short-video recommendation System; dance variants; published clips
  recoveredRelations: change in the recorded relative visibility of the two variants in the recommender; change in the share of each variant among the covered clips
  recoveredClaim: visibility changed and a different mix appeared in the covered published clips
  applicablePatternRefs: C.36 for transmission or retention; C.28 for a causal comparison
  retainedSourceLabelUse: "platform" denotes the recommender in the visibility claim
  admissibleUse: report the observed change in the clips and identify the question about dancers' choices
  blockedUse: attributing a change in copying or in the classes’ repertoire to the recommender on these records alone
  nextUseOrStop: publish the supported account now, or obtain evidence about the dancers if the intended claim depends on it
```

#### C.36.P:5.2 - "This Tradition Is An Attractor"

If `attractor` is a loose metaphor for a stable recognizable style, state that local meaning and use C.36 for the cultural-evolution case. Use F.9 only when a relation between distinct source-local cells is current. For a basin, stable-dynamics, or state-transition-law claim, recover the model, correspondence and conditions that support it. Use an adequate domain result directly, A.3.3 for its state space and transition law, C.27 for a temporal-adequacy question, and C.29 when a separate representation, transfer, or reliance question remains.

#### C.36.P:5.3 - "Technique As Developmental Machinery"

If technique names a way of doing work, use `A.3.1 U.Method`. If it names a training plan, use `A.15.2 U.WorkPlan`. For a claim about a particular rehearsal or production occurrence, establish A.13's basis and independent A.15.1 admission; use F.6 only for precise assignment-bound attribution. Ordinary guidance about rehearsing or producing needs no invented occurrence. If the same term carries different source-local meanings, preserve each meaning and its claims. Use F.17 when their identification or qualification is needed, F.18 only when a durable name is needed, and F.9 only when a relation between distinct cells is current. Use C.36 only when the technique participates in a cultural-evolution case.

#### C.36.P:5.4 - "The Scene Became A New Level"

If a music or dance source says a scene, platform circulation, or canon “became a new level”, first recover the claim. Have participants become constituents of a new performing collective, has circulation reached more of the existing groups, or does a model retain fewer distinctions for the same question? Identify the actual composition, coverage or model change and the evidence for it. Wider circulation can itself be the whole claim. Use C.36 for a changed recognition regime, C.18 for an archive relation, G.5 for a selected-set result declaration, G.11 for refresh, and F.17, F.18, or F.9 for term and bridge work. A whole-reidentification or MHT claim uses B.2.P and then B.2 or its System or episteme specialization when current. A feedback-down claim uses B.2.5. A frustration or residual claim uses C.30.ILC for an architecture residual, C.29 for a mathematical lens, and D.3 or D.4 for ethical level conflict or mediation.
