### C.37.MM:4 - Solution

#### C.37.MM:4.1 - Establish what the models must support together

Name the intended inference, action or reproduction task and its possible outcomes. Identify which model supplies which premise. For each premise recover the modeled entity, conditions, interpretation and relevant edition. A local name for several models supplies a convenient reference. If the proposed **multimodel** is to be relied on as a coordinated set, establish the consequential connections that let the models support this use.

When several models concern the same entity, establish its identity across them. When they concern different entities, state the actual relation. A system, the work that produces it and a cost estimate for that work can all affect one design decision. The estimate's use depends on what work it covers. For an admitted holon, B.1 and the kind-specific method govern the inference from the constituents and their organization to a whole-level result. A modeled relation with independently identified participants needs no presumption of physical parts.

##### C.37.MM:4.1.1 - Choose and connect the required ranges

Start from the composition scales and model distinctions the decision calls for. If choosing a range is part of your task, use C.39.SA:4. If the intended recipient must choose, make the method and evidence for that choice available to them. In a multimodel, establish what each model contributes and how those contributions can hold together.

1. Identify the types of entities and actions that can affect that result. For each relevant kind of holon, follow its own composition as far as the decision needs. A system has integrated components. A composite Method admits particular Methods under its construction rule. For composite dated Work, establish which actual performances did a constitutive part of that very job. An overlapping or earlier performance can help without belonging to it. For an episteme, identify the claims and grounds incorporated into the account and any external sources to which it appeals. For a discipline, identify the knowledge and practice its working arrangement integrates. A part, an available alternative and an external supplier can play different parts in the decision.
2. For each inference, identify what its model can leave open. Which alternatives must remain distinguishable for this inquiry? Can a general rule settle it throughout the stated range? Use B.5.MR when comparing models towards greater abstraction or refinement. You can change the scope of composition while holding model abstraction fixed, or compare models of the same whole while holding its composition in view.
3. Connect the results. Name who or what participates and the relation on which the inference depends. Work enacts a Method. A system participates in the Work. A report relies on an observation made during that Work. These links may be needed together. A rule at every eligible scale, an influence across scales and a result of several working together each need grounds for the connection they assert. A control layer adds its own functional organization when the decision depends on it.
4. Trace the explanation forward to the recipient's decision. Could all the local claims stay true while a different connection changed that decision? If so, recover the missing connection when it is available. If it is unavailable, give the warranted partial answer and identify the condition that still decides the original question. A partial result can finish the commission when it already settles the recipient’s criterion. For an assertion covering a range, show why the grounds hold throughout it. One governing rule or a sufficient bound can sometimes settle the entire question.

For example, a receiving shift needs to know whether two language desks can jointly issue consistent stop instructions today. The desks are components of one publishing service. Comparing the stop clauses belongs to the release Method. Identify the reviews actually performed as contributions to this release. Check that each signed report covers the translation now ready to issue and identifies its applicable source edition. An earlier, independent review of those same texts can support their consistency when the release terms accept it. That reliance need not make the earlier review part of today’s Work. Both reviews can be correct while one source calls for a stop before the next operation and the other calls for it afterwards. Following the reports back to their sources reveals the conflict. If the sources differ only elsewhere, both translations convey the same applicable stop condition, and the commission accepts that equivalence, consistency is established. Suppose that both approved texts are ready, the desks have authority to issue them, the required staff and channels are available to both in the agreed release window today, and these are the remaining release conditions. The desks can jointly issue the consistent instructions in that window. If one desk has no usable route to the shift before the deadline, joint issue in that window is unavailable even when the instructions agree. A claim that the shift actually received them needs evidence of delivery.

The general result is a connected account of the conditions for one receiving action. The principle about agreement applies across disciplines. Editorial work makes the relation concrete through the applicable clauses, a local release procedure can further restrict which editions qualify, and one actual issue supplies evidence about identified texts and reviews. A larger service could still be examined on one occasion. The number or size of its desks assigns no level of model abstraction. Keep the account's declared types and ranges when applying the method to a different action. C.39.QR:4.1 gives the return from an example to that commission.

#### C.37.MM:4.2 - Test the joint conclusion

State how the claims from the models are to be read together. Align such matters as units, conditions, time, configuration and whether a value was stipulated, observed or predicted when the conclusion depends on them. Check whether the commitments can all hold in the same case. Pairwise compatibility can leave the whole set inconsistent. If no case satisfies all the commitments, trace the conflict to the models or their interpretation. If information is missing, identify the premise or correspondence needed to settle compatibility.

To establish that an outcome is possible under these models, show that at least one jointly admissible case has that outcome. You can exhibit such a case or prove that it exists. If the recipient needs a setting they can actually use, recover one and check it against the conditions. To guarantee the outcome under the stated conditions, establish that at least one case is admissible and that every jointly admissible case has that outcome. One admissible exception defeats the guarantee.

Ask whether the outstanding uncertainty affects the decision you owe. Two interpretations can yield different estimates and still put every admissible result on the same side of a decision threshold. That can settle the choice while the estimate remains open. If no fixed choice works throughout the allowed conditions, establish whether the recipient may choose after a distinguishing observation. The observation must be available before commitment and the action for each possible answer must work throughout the cases still compatible with it. If those conditions fail, return the unresolved choice or the demonstrated limit. An incomplete list of interpretations can still support a conclusion if every permitted interpretation must preserve a sufficient bound or other relevant property. Where no grounds cover the others, qualify the answer to the interpretations examined.

A claim about a candidate is distinct from a claim about how an actual system performed. To use a test result from an observed system, state what was observed and which target claim it supports under which conditions. B.5.MR supplies the test for a further claim that joint use strictly refines the earlier model. A gain in interpretation can occur while the set of eligible candidates stays the same.

#### C.37.MM:4.3 - Make the receiving use reproducible

Ask what the successor will need to do. To **read** an existing result, they need the governing types, meanings, constraints, editions and consequential links. A **metamodel** can supply conventions that determine which models are admissible. A **megamodel**, in this handover use, makes the related models and the relevant links to their governing models recoverable. A reference to an accessible, identified edition suffices when it gives the recipient what they need. If the governing conventions may themselves change, preserve the grounds for the relevant higher-order choices.

To **repeat, adapt or examine the original derivation**, identify its production method and the inputs and assumptions on which it depends. To **check its conclusion independently**, establish the common target, conditions and meaning of the result. A missing premise leaves the original derivation incomplete. The conclusion can still be established by another sound method. For a claim about every admissible case, one counterexample that meets its conditions refutes the claim. A glossary can settle what an estimate means while leaving the successor unable to recompute it. When one source supplies a well-understood method, name the applicable edition and the values fixed locally.

To **act on the result**, the recipient may additionally need the resources, capability and authority for the work. An assignment, where relied on, is a relation with a system as its holder under A.2.1. Handing over an account does not establish that anyone has performed the work.

#### C.37.MM:4.4 - Reopen only the dependent conclusions

When an observation, model edition or interpretation changes, follow the consequential relations to the conclusions and actions that used it. Return the changed answer and the affected uses to the intended recipient. Inform other users when the undertaking or a standing duty calls for it. Establish the revised answer under the conditions that matter, using the available grounds or obtaining a new check when needed or commissioned. If an earlier model is retained as evidence of a past decision, keep its effective edition identifiable.
