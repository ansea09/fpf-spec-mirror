### E.10.ARCH:5 - Direct known claim-rule use

If the governed object, exact direct relation, claim-bearing episteme, representation use, or claim kind and its defining or testing rule are recoverable by value, use that rule directly and cite the pattern containing it. Do not send direct `C.30`, `C.16`, `C.29`, `E.21`, `E.18`, `A.10`, `A.3.1`, `A.3.2`, `A.6.0`, `A.6.1`, `E.20`, `A.15.PROD`, evidence, assurance, gate, Work, decision, causal-use, release, naming, controlled-coarsening, action-invitation, `A.6.M` module-interface, publication-face, or mathematical-lens claims through a restoration pattern merely because a familiar trigger word appears. Use `A.6.P.WMR` only while the exact Work and Method boundary relation or exact non-assertability result remains hidden. Keep known-predicate failure, unavailable fact, and absent rule or declaration separately `factually unsupported`, `missing-information`, and `missing-governor`; only the last is a blocker that names the needed future relation rule or declaration.

Apply `E.10.DEV` only while development or evolution wording still hides its changed or represented subject, needed continuity or membership, posture, direction or value basis, direct owner, or receiving use; continue to `E.10.MOVE` only for an independent path ambiguity. Apply `E.10.ROLE` only when bare claim-bearing *role* still hides its object or relation. Apply `A.6.RSIR`, `A.6.P`, `A.6.P.WMR`, `A.6.F`, `C.2.P`, `C.2.P.DR`, `C.30.P`, `C.30.STRAT`, `C.16.P`, `C.16.Q`, `A.19.SPR`, or `A.3.1` only when wording hides the EntityOfConcern under repair, direct relation use, system-role assignment, signature declaration, interface claim, declaration-local SlotKind, characteristic, scale, score, quality characterization, comparison reference set, source-relation disposition, state-family value, method-side governed object, Work and Method boundary claim, declarative-representation use, admissible use, or remaining reader use.
