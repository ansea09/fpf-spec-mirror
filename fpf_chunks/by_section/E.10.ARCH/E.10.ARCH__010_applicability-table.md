**Architecture source-word recovery note.** When architecture prose says that a source, document, view, ADR, diagram, dashboard, model, publication face, or source-return condition carries an architecture claim, do not mint an architecture-local `Source` kind. Use `C.2.P` first only while source expression, publication construction, carrier-relation construction, source relation, project-side reference, or non-use disposition is still hidden. After recovery, the subject pattern is selected declaratively: `C.30.P` for architecture or structure wording, `C.30.AD` for architecture-description and source-return use, `C.30.ASV` for structural-view adequacy, `C.32` for architecture synthesis or decision claims, or the subject pattern for direct evidence, assurance, gate, work, decision, publication, or currentness when that is the actual claim.
