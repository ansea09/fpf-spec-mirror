### B.2.3:7 - Common Anti-Patterns and How to Avoid Them

| Anti-pattern | Symptom | Repair |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Collection as MHT | Aggregation is taken as sufficient for an episteme-result MHT. | Recover the candidate and test A.1 plus C.2.1 constitution and the B.2 comparison; if recognition fails, withhold episteme-result recognition. Keep any independently established collection or ordinary episteme claim without inferring that it is sufficient for the use; report `unknown` when a required test cannot be evaluated. |
| PDF as episteme | A PDF carrier or its publication form is used as the theory itself. | Use publication patterns for the PDF and keep B.2's `resultHolonRef` for the independently constituted episteme. |
| Doctrine receives agency | "The standard enforces..." or "the theory decides..." | For a literal claim of dated enforcement or decision Work, recover its performer through A.13 and admit the Work independently under A.15.1; add F.6 only when the use consumes precise assignment-bound attribution. Otherwise use F.19 to retain ordinary guidance wording when its capable user and action are recoverable. |
| Morphism as MHT | A view, translation, coarsening, or retargeting is taken as sufficient for B.2 whole reidentification. | Use A.6.2, A.6.3, A.6.4 or A.6.3.CSC for that operation and C.2.1 for episteme identity; use B.2 for a remaining whole-reidentification question. |
| Synthesis as high trust | A new theory inherits trust because its sources were reliable. | When this assurance claim is needed, assess it under B.3 and retain the source-use relations. Use A.10 for a needed replayable source-to-use account, B.3.5 only when its profile is elected, and C.2.1 for a live constitution question. Reuse adequate existing support rather than inheriting assurance from the sources. |
