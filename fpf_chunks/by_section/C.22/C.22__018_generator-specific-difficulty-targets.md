### C.22:15 - Generator-specific difficulty targets

When a declared search or learning policy seeks feasible but challenging tasks, state the feasibility and difficulty criteria for that task family in the policy. Use C.19 when the question is treatment of a still-live pool, and C.18 for the generation or archive account. Difficulty alone does not establish that a TaskSignature is informative. An ordinary signature includes only traits needed by its receiving use, with no general requirement to make the task harder. G.1 applies when authoring a reusable generator/selector kit; it supplies no universal task-difficulty target.
