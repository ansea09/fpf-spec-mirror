### C.39.QR:1 - Problem frame

**Use this when** choosing what work would answer a question, or bringing a result back from a part, model, example, sample, summary or delegated contribution. A question can change in the first reply, before any partial result exists. A later handover can carry a sound piece of work into a conclusion about a different subject. In either situation, recover what the recipient needs to understand, obtain or do and how the proposed work can supply it.

Recover the original subject and required result, explain what the intermediate work establishes, and supply the grounds for returning that result to the original use. Where the return exposes missing work, develop that contribution or obtain its supplier. The first useful result is a warranted answer to the original question, or an account of what has been obtained and what still has to be done.

Use an already adequate direct answer. A local operation can finish under its own established criterion when the assignment is local. This Method is useful where a consequential return remains uncertain; ordinary continuation under unchanged, understood conditions needs no repeated reconstruction.
