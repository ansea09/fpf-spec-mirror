### E.9.DA:6 - Bias annotation

This pattern favors settling decisions before drafting. A missing decision costs more to repair once several pattern authors depend on it.

A small editorial decision can use `E.9` directly. In an ordinary DRR review, judge the decision, perform the bounded omitted-question search, and identify the first action. This review needs no separate assessment record. When a named later reliance needs an exact reusable result, Method, application, and Work remain independently conditional. If Work is asserted, identify the actual performer Systems at the claimed scale, the Method enacted and the independent A.15.1 basis for each Work claimed. An application, attribution, result, evidence use, or receiving status enters through its own claim and grounds. `E.21` covers pattern quality, `E.23` repeated improvement, and `F.19` wording repair, with `E.10` for cues and unresolved meanings.
