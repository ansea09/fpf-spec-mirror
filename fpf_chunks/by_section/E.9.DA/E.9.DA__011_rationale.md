### E.9.DA:10 - Rationale

Settle a missing FPF decision in the DRR before distribution so several later authors do not have to make the same choice. For an ordinary review, judge the decision in the DRR using the bounded search for an omitted question and any actual-host replay the decision requires.

A separately requested reusable evaluation or named later reliance can require a complete coordinate result and exact evaluation or result identities. These two review forms let authors keep decision records as concise as the decisions allow and supply the evaluation detail that reliance needs. In either form, adequacy depends on the content of the decision.
