### B.5.4:9 - Consequences

The practitioner obtains an interpretation they can use and revise. A mismatch becomes a question about an object, relation, condition or domain account. A later calculation receives identified quantities; a model receives interpretable elements; further inquiry receives a next question or observation to pursue.

The cost is constructing and examining the correspondence. This work can be small when the relations are clear.
