### C.39.WI:9 - Consequences

Method improvement becomes answerable to work that matters and to the cost of obtaining the improvement. Adequate Methods and useful source contributions survive, while missing explanations and provision are less likely to be mistaken for each other. A saving outside the output constraint can be selected on its merits.

The work needs professional judgement and enough evidence to discriminate its serious alternatives. Discovery and measurement consume resources. A useful result can be a justified stop, a better provision arrangement or a retained possibility rather than a new Method or publication.
