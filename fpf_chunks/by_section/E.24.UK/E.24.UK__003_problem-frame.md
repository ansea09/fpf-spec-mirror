### E.24.UK:1 - Problem Frame

FPF reserves `U.*` names for admitted durable U-kinds. Current source material and older corpus passages can still place that spelling on a declaration-local SlotKind, participant designation, selected structure, publication form, representation element, or unsettled candidate. The spelling is therefore evidence of admission pressure, not evidence of admission.

Section 4.2 separates exact accepted admission-result references from open prerequisites, blocked candidates, and non-admission exits. A public spelling, owner citation, or orientation row supplies no admission by itself. Existing root and same-individual-dependent kinds remain usable only through the accepted result reference recorded there. `U.Capability` is rejected for the recovered A.2.2 need because qualified holder-ability claims require no additional kind. An assertion cannot settle an unresolved prerequisite kind.

E.24.UK governs the following distinctions. A world-side relation participant keeps its independently governed kind. A `RelationSignature` SlotKind stays declaration-local. An assertion-side designation stays in its claim-bearing episteme. A publication form or C.29 representation keeps its direct use.

E.24.UK is an E.24 subpattern because kind admission and ontology disposition share one decision basis. A justified kind admission does not require an additional durable ontic; E.24 separately tests whether such coordination is needed. E.24 remains the head pattern for `U.Ontic` and ontic introduction. E.24.UK governs the detailed U-kind admission rules.
