## A.15.8.MS - Configure Methodological Support for Work

> **Type:** Method pattern
> **Status:** Stable

> **Working result:** an arrangement shown to support the named users’ tasks under the required conditions. A design can be supported by access rules and resource guarantees. A claim about performance needs grounds for what happened, and an order for a trial includes that trial. Where the grounds permit several arrangements or cover only some tasks, report those alternatives or that boundary. Method material, user Systems, participating Systems, relations, Work, results, and gaps remain separate. An A.22 selected structure is returned only when its four identity discriminators obtain for the named use. A claim of dated selection Work needs its own performance basis.
