### C.17:1 - The practitioner route

#### C.17:1.1 - Qualitative first move

Name the bearer and the judgement the reader needs. For novelty, identify a reference set and the supported difference. When the comparison basis is yours to choose, ask whose prior designs, practices or results matter to this judgement and which differences would change the recipient’s use. An inventory can answer a question about its entries. A claim extending beyond it needs grounds that an omitted comparator or a distinction lost by the representation cannot overturn the conclusion. For usefulness, name the objective and the evidence for the practical effect. For eligibility, check the applicable must-criteria. State the limits of each conclusion. The following discussion needs all three judgements.

> Compared with the admitted five-year pump-design set, P-22's inspected split-clamp arrangement is a supported difference. The cited assembly evidence supports shorter assembly than the current shop design P-11, and the design must not require new tooling. The inspection does not support claims about other pump families.

This is a C.17 result for an ordinary design discussion. It does not imply a numerical Novelty value or an overall assessment occurrence.

#### C.17:1.2 - Obtain a coordinate or compare profiles

When the receiver needs a coordinate or a comparison of coordinate profiles under a declared comparator, use the steps that supply its grounds.

1. select one A.19 CharacteristicSpace and its `A.19.ECS` specification;
2. for a corpus-relative claim, fix the finite comparison corpus, inclusion rule, source editions, scope, comparison window and evidence.
3. for a usefulness or eligibility claim, identify the objective, acceptance criterion or applicable must-constraints.
4. identify how each requested value is obtained and the inputs, assumptions or observations it requires.
5. for each coordinate, cite an existing complete `C.16` measurement result, perform and constitute the missing C.16 measurement, or state a `C.2.1` non-measurement ascription under its declared rule;
6. form only the aggregate conclusion needed by the receiving comparison;
7. add an optional profile payload, representation, or record only when a named receiver needs it.

Return only the missing premise that blocks the current coordinate or conclusion. A missing measurement does not invalidate independent qualitative claims or other coordinates.

#### C.17:1.3 - Dated overall-assessment branch

Use this branch when the claim concerns the work of making an overall assessment. Establish which systems performed the relevant work and how their contributions produced the assessment under the admitted Method. A service can supply a coordinate measurement, two assessors can apply different criteria, and a board can adopt their combined judgment. Each attribution requires grounds for that contribution. For a particular dated `U.Work`, use A.13 and A.15.1 at the claimed scale, with the Method under A.3.1. Apply F.6 where attribution to a particular assignment matters. A requirement for independent assessments remains in force. A receiving question about the value alone may be settled by a reliable existing result.

Do not infer an A.6.1 operation application from the Method, Work, configuration, or result. If a receiving claim separately asserts an exact operation application or binding, satisfy the current A.6.1 application account and cite that application. Otherwise retain only the evaluator contributions, assignments, Method enactments, dated assessment Work and results that the claim needs.
