### C.20:8 - Consequences

**Benefits.** The construction account distinguishes constituent replacement from reidentification without freezing a universal canon or Method repertoire. It preserves rival traditions through their explicit contributions and admissible directed uses. Comparison, selection and publication can use that account and their own characteristic, evidence or authority grounds; they do not supply missing construction facts.

**Costs and trade-offs.** A positive discipline claim needs exact parts, direct relation intervals, whole-forming claims, an assembly, a reidentification rule, one whole characteristic, and a larger-assembly compatibility account. Ordinary use can state these in readable sentences. A comparison adds its comparison basis and lawful operation; a selected CG-Spec adds that interface's declarations. Cross-local use that relies on an obtaining semantic Bridge adds its bounded-use claim and reliance grounds. Trace, publication, health, selector or assurance apparatus is added for the receiving use that needs it.

**Risks avoided.** The pattern prevents a field from being inferred from a label, identity from an institution, parts from publication, breadth from context counts, merger from a Bridge, ontology from a health score, and method or discipline membership from a registry.
