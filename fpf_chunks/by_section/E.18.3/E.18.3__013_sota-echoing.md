As of 2026-07-11, OCPQ is the current research comparator for typed multi-object constraint structure, while Modelica 3.7 and Dyad 3.1.0 are current engineering comparators for relation-first models separated from analyses and execution. The older CMMN, Declare, DCR, and artifact-centric rows supply lineage. These source decisions changed `4.0` by requiring exact typed relations before continuation, `4.1` by keeping independently identified neighboring values and separately supported claims explicit, `4.2` by preserving graph-shaped alternatives behind a linear demonstration, and the physical case by separating structure from work and analysis. Reopen the adoptions when object-centric constraint methods change object-relation treatment, model languages change model-analysis separation, or use evidence shows that these distinctions no longer prevent workflow, query-result, or execution-artifact overread.
