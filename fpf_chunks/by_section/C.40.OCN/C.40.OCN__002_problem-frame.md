### C.40.OCN:1 - Problem frame

Use this Method when an agent or arrangement of capable contributors is exploring what useful future Work could become possible for a named holder, but the problem, contribution or direction is not yet fixed. A new technology, an emerging use, a changed constraint or dissatisfaction with familiar options may prompt the inquiry. The result describes the proposed contribution and its conditions clearly enough to be examined outside the original inquiry. A list of fashionable subjects leaves that work undone.

The subject is a useful possible contribution and the conditions under which it could become obtainable. This Method governs how a possible direction is made meaningful and conditionally reachable. It does not govern an adviser’s recommendation, a development intervention, strategic commitment, procurement or the eventual choice.

A separate adviser is not required. A person exploring future engineering Work can begin here and stop with an opportunity or a question to pursue next. If another performer later gives advice to a named recipient, the applicable advising Method supplies the engagement and professional conditions; the decision rule and authority govern the later choice.

Do not use this Method to reopen an adequate option set merely because more ideas could be generated. Use the choice or comparison Method when the worthwhile result and live alternatives are already sufficient. Use a qualified specialist Method when it owns the whole question. An evolutionary account of a population without a recipient is not a development-direction choice; a researcher's intervention question needs its own science and authority basis.

A complete first return can be modest: “This contribution could address the named receiving-use problem, but only if access to these records and the required review support can be obtained. The next question is whether the data owner can provide that bounded access.” Missing feasibility does not have to erase the useful problem; it prevents a stronger reachability claim.

The first useful result is a candidate opportunity with a worthwhile problem or proposed promise, dependencies, bounded reachability rationale, uncertainty and a next discriminating question.
