### E.4.FPF:10 - Rationale

FPF carries transdisciplinary first-principles distinctions that can seed and discipline many domain and local frameworks. Those frameworks supply their domain or local subjects and practices.

These cross-domain uses make FPF form an architecture concern. Separate publication units, forms, exact presentation carriers, access routes, and the framework edition so adoption work returns authority to the subject patterns. `E.4.DPF.DA` answers the DPF package question. Use `E.21` for individual patterns and `E.2.DA` for whole-FPF adequacy.
