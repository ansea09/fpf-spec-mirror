### C.38:1 - Problem frame

**Plain name.** Turn labels such as *build*, *buy*, *reuse*, *provider*, *internal team*, *outsource*, or *AI* into comparable complete ways of getting the same result.

**Use this when.** An appraisal, construction or choice calls for comparable ways of obtaining one named result. The proposals may mix performers, Systems, Methods, commercial labels and enabling conditions. Establish what each whole way could deliver on the receiving terms before presenting the comparison or using it to choose.

**Reader.** The practitioner preparing decision-ready alternatives for one receiver. The pattern forms candidates; it neither chooses for the receiver nor realizes a retained way.

**First useful result.** A comparison establishes what the proposed ways can supply on the same receiving terms, which differences matter and which premises remain open. Two labels can describe the same way. A finding that one or none of the named ways meets the terms can finish their appraisal. An order to construct additional ways remains open until they are supplied or their impossibility within the permitted range is established. Where `C.11` applies, use the resulting `OptionSet` to derive a supported disposition. Its `ChoiceResult` may be advice to the chooser. To report an effective decision, establish the act or delegated procedure that gave it effect. Where realization is due, return the retained way to the direct realization Method. Identify the first unsupported branch if it prevents the work.

**What changes in practice.** Instead of comparing *buy* against *AI* or *internal* as if the labels were peer kinds, the practitioner asks how each whole way could make the same accepted result available. Duplicate labels merge, materially different ways under one label split, and omitted integration, evidence, support, capability, custody, resource, consequence, and exit burdens become visible before commitment.

**When to stop.** If the receiver can already obtain the ordered result on the agreed terms and no further choice or appraisal is due under the applicable commission, show how the existing provision meets those terms and omit the proposed new work. If one authorized way suffices and no comparison is due, use the direct domain Method. A domain comparison that already answers the decision can go straight to the chooser. For a choice, follow the governing decision method. Use `C.11` when it governs selection from the complete `OptionSet`.

**Use another pattern for these questions:**

- `A.15.9` for one missing or unqualified bounded result from another practice;
- `C.39` for a missing obtaining explanation;
- `C.40` for developing and examining material, including its coupled entry when problems and ways must change together;
- `C.18` for archive, front or possibility-space claims;
- `C.19` for live-pool exploration policy;
- `C.32` for architecture-candidate synthesis;
- `A.15.8` for one actual-Work or present-WorkPlan support configuration; and
- the direct domain Method for realization, production, provision, contracting, acceptance, or use.
