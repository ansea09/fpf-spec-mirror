### C.38:7 - Conformance and practical checks

A use conforms only when the checks needed by its claimed comparison pass:

1. The result, receiver, use, horizon and acceptance conditions are explicit. Existing provision is assessed when it bears on this question. The assessment identifies the committing party when authority affects feasibility or an actual commitment is due. If the commission includes the receiver’s later capability, the comparison identifies the work that would establish it and the assistance allowed.
2. The alternatives address the same receiving result. Ground their material differences, what rules any out, and any claim that the permitted range is exhausted.
3. Every decision-bearing premise is visibly supported now, proposed, or unknown; proposed or unknown premises establish no actual capability, authority, Work, provision, delivery, acceptance, availability, or use.
4. The same decision-changing parity questions are applied to every way; an omitted group is justified by irrelevance to this choice.
5. A decision-reversing unknown remains a gap or probe. Protected conditions are not hidden inside one unexplained scalar.
6. `A.19.CPM` or `A.19.SelectorMechanism` is used only when its evidence-gating, incomparability, abstention, or set-return contribution is needed.
7. Include only complete-enough whole ways in a finite `OptionSet`. When a choice is due, the chooser makes it under the applicable decision method. A choice under `C.11` yields its `ChoiceResult`. The commissioned appraisal can finish when it establishes what the proposed ways would deliver.
8. The rule for retaining the comparison prevents a duplicate episteme when an owning domain result already carries the complete comparison.
9. A retained way names any first unsupported realization branch or the direct Method that can proceed; the comparison itself claims no realization or later Work.
10. The reopen condition names a changed premise or result that can alter the comparison or choice.

**Recognition check.** The practitioner can recover one common result from a *build/buy/provider/AI* list and identify a decision-relevant hidden difference or duplicate. An arrangement taxonomy is unnecessary.

**Assurance check.** For a consequential use, inspect each actual premise under its direct evidence and authority patterns, examine any plausible omitted burden that could reverse the choice, and verify that the selected domain assurance and acceptance Methods remain outside `C.38`.
