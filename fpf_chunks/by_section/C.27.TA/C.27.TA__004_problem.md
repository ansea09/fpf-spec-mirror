### C.27.TA:2 - Problem

Common failures:

1. **Cadence and rhythm lack a bearer or timing.** A text says "release cadence" or "team rhythm" without naming the bearer, timing reference, or the extent to which the claim applies.
2. **Freshness lacks a reference date or use condition.** A source, benchmark, dashboard, or claim is called current without a reference date or the conditions that make it current for the intended use.
3. **Recovery and stabilization hide their interval.** A claim says "recover faster" or "stabilize" without saying after which disturbance, over what duration, and for which bearer.
4. **Effort and inertia lack a subject or course of change.** A text speaks about momentum, residue, stored work, adaptation cost, or resistance without linking it to the relevant object and course of change.
5. **Transformation statements leave timing implicit.** A transformation statement names a change but leaves timing and ordering implicit, so method, mechanism, work, evidence, and temporal claims get tangled.
