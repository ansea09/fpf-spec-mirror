### C.41.QC:12 - Relations

C.41 supplies proposed work questions and receives coverage corrections. C.41.TF preserves qualified meanings, C.41.OF makes observations accessible, C.41.UA performs checks, and C.41.SR interprets their combined use. [FPF A.19.ULSAM](https://github.com/ailev/FPF/blob/main/FPF-Spec.md#a19ulsam---unified-lawful-scale-aggregation-mechanism) is a conditional return when the receiving use needs numerical aggregation; ordinary separate answers require no such calculation.

[C.41](https://github.com/ailev/FPF/blob/main/FPF-Spec.md#c41---select-checklist-questions-for-a-work-result) supplies construction of work questions and their criteria in §§4.1–4.2; [C.39.RQ](https://github.com/ailev/FPF/blob/main/FPF-Spec.md#c39rq---construct-situated-method-requirements) supplies criteria for a required Method contribution. [A.3.1.WT](https://github.com/ailev/FPF/blob/main/FPF-Spec.md#a31wt---design-and-interpret-a-method-work-trial) supplies representative trial, and [C.11.MV](https://github.com/ailev/FPF/blob/main/FPF-Spec.md#c11mv---compare-the-worth-of-a-method-option) worth appraisal. The subject examination supplies the observation or judgement whose adequacy is being tested; administrative claim checking is one domain example. Qualification uses the required results for the uncertainty in checklist criteria or means; it does not replace those Methods.
