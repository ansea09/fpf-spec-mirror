### A.2.9:12 — Relations

**Uses / builds on**

* Uses **A.13** for the actual performer's agency basis and **A.15.1 (`U.Work`)** for independent admission of the communicative occurrence. Uses **F.6** only afterward for a needed precise `performedUnderAssignment` claim through the same obtaining assignment. Uses a separate optional `methodDescriptionRef` only when the receiving claim needs that episteme.
* Uses **A.7** for the actual-act≠record/description≠carrier split.
* Coordinates with **A.2.6** for scope/window discipline.
* **F.18** can serve as a **lexical entry point** for naming `U.SpeechAct` and “utterance” in the promise/utterance/commitment triad.

**Used by**

* **A.2.8 (`U.Commitment`)** when this act supplies all or part of the required basis for an independently obtaining commitment, and **A.2.8.PER** when this act alone or together with other acts under the applicable rule institutes an independently obtaining `GrantedPermissionRelation@Context`.
* **A.2.5 (assignment-state predicates and their Work-admission use)** when the selected state predicate or direct consumer requires an authorization or approval act.
* **A.6.C** for unpacking promise, approval, guarantee, and agreement-like boundary wording while preventing episteme-as-agent claims and preserving provenance.
