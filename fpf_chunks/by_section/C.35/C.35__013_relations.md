### C.35:12 - Relations

- **Builds on:** `C.30`, `C.30.AD`, `C.30.ASV`, `A.22`, `C.32.P2S`, and `C.32`.
- **Uses:** `C.34` when the transformation branch or an explicit baseline comparison must preserve selected source structure; `C.33` when capture and loss in the output are the current issue; `C.29` when choosing, transferring, or relying on a mathematical representation remains unresolved.

**Coordinates with:**

| Pattern | Coordination |
| --- | --- |
| `A.3.4` | Each actual bounded change. |
| `A.13`, `A.15.1`, `A.2.1`, and `F.6` | `A.13` supplies actual performer assignment bases, then independent `A.15.1` governs performed generation or discovery Work; `A.2.1` concerns the assignments, and `F.6` applies only when precise assignment-bound attribution is current. |
| `A.15.PROD` and `A.6.RCD` | Production or Work-to-change claims. |
| `C.36` | A cultural-evolution case supplies the generated or discovered result; C.36 retains governance of that case. |

C.35 also coordinates with `C.30.STRAT`, `C.30.TFS-REL`, `A.6.M`, `C.31`, `C.31.ASAP`, `C.32.ACS`, `C.32.ACE`, `C.16`, `C.25`, `G.5`, `C.18`, `C.19`, `E.18`, `C.32.PAD`, and `C.32.ADR`.

- **Boundary:** C.35 governs admission of a generated or discovered result for architecture use before or around C.32 candidate admission. It does not build the candidate palette, select from alternatives, govern reusable generators, define eval programs, measure values, decide projects, supply evidence or assurance, authorize work, publish the result, or prove realization.
