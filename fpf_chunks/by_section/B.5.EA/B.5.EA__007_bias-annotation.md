### B.5.EA:6 - Bias-Annotation

The listener's expertise can make a premature explanation attractive. Preserve the participant's opportunity to reject it. Conversely, a personally satisfying phrase can resist correction; another use or observation may expose a limitation even when the phrase feels fitting.
