### B.5.EA:10 - Rationale

The operation between noticing and reasoning is often left implicit. Preserving a cue prevents loss; forming a model requires distinctions with which one can work. Articulation connects these contributions without treating a preliminary expression as either worthless or established.

Meaning can change during formulation. Retaining the ability to revisit the originating experience allows that change to be examined. The practical aim is a contribution to inquiry or action, including a better question.
