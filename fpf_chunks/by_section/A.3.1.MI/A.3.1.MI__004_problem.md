### A.3.1.MI:2 - Problem

A rollout plan can be reported as adoption. Training can be reported as capability. Tool availability can be reported as use. A manager's sponsorship can be reported as permission or authority. Later Work can be merged with the introduction Work, and a favourable domain result can be assigned to the Method while another change could account for it.

When several positions are compressed into one intervention label, the team cannot revise locally. It either repeats the whole programme or rejects it, even when the defect lies in one description, support relation, capability condition, assignment, permission, authority boundary, or outside-result assumption.
