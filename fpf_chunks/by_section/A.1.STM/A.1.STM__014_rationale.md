### A.1.STM:12 - Rationale

The project-role and use account makes division of labour intelligible: it lets a participant explain why a local choice matters to people outside that task. Receiving changes and the wider functioning at the operating time give reasons for the required contribution. That contribution constrains plausible construction; construction constrains the Methods and capabilities needed to obtain it. These dependencies explain the mantra's order. A complaint can lead back to the same grounds, while proactive examination can reveal an unanswered question before its consequences are felt. Criticism and experience explain the returns.

The same reasoning applies recursively to builders without making creation a composition relation or every builder a Work performer. Separating the project role from System identity preserves this organizing principle across different projects and before a holder exists. Separating local completion from receiving use lets a team finish bounded work without mistaking that completion for success of the whole undertaking.
