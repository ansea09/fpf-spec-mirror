### F.19:10 - Rationale

Precise plain language has two obligations. The sentence must be semantically complete enough to recover its predicates, participants, referents, kinds, and operational detail. Every additional structure must also earn its place by changing understanding or use for the intended reader.

Either obligation alone is insufficient: a fully typed sentence can still be noise, and a short sentence can still hide its object.

Recover the governing message and relations, remove unsupported structure and displaced apparatus, then write the shortest ordinary sentence that preserves the live meaning. Use `E.10` for cues and unresolved FPF wording. `F.19` supplies the ordinary connected reading. Attention management remains outside the language pattern.
