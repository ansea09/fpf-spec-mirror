### E.22:10 - Rationale

A generic request leaves the evaluator to choose what counts as quality. To show which question a quality result answers, bind its object version, selected characteristic space, predicate and any comparator, one evaluation ClaimScope, consuming work or decision, evaluation pattern, any separately identified semantic Method, purpose, expected evidence basis, expected result form, and boundary.

When needed, the question frame also states a question-changing evaluator condition or intended-evaluator identity. The pattern, Method, assignment, descriptions, dated evaluation Work, evidence use, result, decision and project authority each retain their own grounds.
