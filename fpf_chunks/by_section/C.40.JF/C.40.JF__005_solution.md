### C.40.JF:4 - Solution

Qualify the necessary conditions together for one named transition or combination. Keep the work proportional to the claim that someone will rely on; do not turn every possible dependency into a new investigation.

#### C.40.JF:4.1 - Bind the claim before testing its dependencies

Name the subject, receiving use, actual or currently relied-on starting configuration, proposed finite change, candidate configuration, horizon, and protected conditions. Recover an adequate direction/support description when one exists. For a path, name the step and the starting configuration it inherits.

State the claim being qualified: one direction alone, several directions together, a sequence, or one branch under a stated contingency. For one possible outcome, establish the existence of a compatible route. To promise a response to any allowed choice, establish who decides, what they can know by each irreversible step and how their rule works across the allowed combinations. To exclude the entire opportunity, show that the obstacle or combination of obstacles covers all permitted routes. “A or B is available” does not establish “A and B can both be realized,” and two separately possible endpoints do not establish a possible transition between them.

Select dependencies by consequence. Ask what absence or failure would block the proposed transition, breach a protection, or materially change its contribution. Include dependencies on capability, access, evidence, acceptance, support, time, resources, authority, and continuing Work only where they affect this claim. An unstated material dependency is a gap, not a satisfied condition.

#### C.40.JF:4.2 - Use the supplying practice's qualified result

For each material dependency, state the required result and why it matters to this transition. Use [A.15.9 — Request and Use a Bounded Result from Another Practice](https://github.com/ailev/FPF/blob/main/FPF-Spec.md) to inspect an existing result and use it at its supported strength for this receiving question. Retain a conditional direction where the needed premise is absent. Select a further request through A.15.9 and C.11.DUA only when its obtainable contribution warrants formulation, qualification, performance, delivery, interpretation, delay, and displaced work.

A provider's offer, a published MethodDescription, and a qualified result about this subject answer different questions. Recover the result's subject and configuration, characteristic or condition, receiving use, any evidence window, limitations, and uncertainty. If new Work is needed, the supplying practice retains its Method, competent performer, authority, and conclusion. Establish the source and support required for this premise use, reusing an adequate existing account. Use A.10 when the source-to-use account remains implicit or must be replayable.

Use ordinary wording that preserves the difference between supported, conditional, unknown, and ruled out. Keep these distinctions at the claim they affect. If authorized access is absent, an access-dependent route cannot be described as available. That fact need not defeat a contribution obtainable without that access.

An existing result for another holder or configuration is not automatically a result for this one. Use the practice's qualified transfer basis, covering the receiving bearer and configuration, characteristic meaning and scale, intended use, evidence window, and uncertainty as required by C.40.OC. Otherwise leave the receiving premise unknown.

#### C.40.JF:4.3 - Test the combination, not just its ingredients

Put jointly necessary conditions on one compatible basis. Recover the time intervals, resource quantities, operating and transition demands, availability restrictions, and protection limits. Before adding the demands on one provider, establish which operations must be performed separately and which one performance can supply to several recipients. Count shared work once and each separate demand in the time window in which it must be met. If sharing is undecided and would change feasibility, keep the two outcomes open.

Ask which conditions compete, complement, substitute for, or interfere with one another. Inspect common causes and shared suppliers, thresholds, congestion, delays, continuing obligations, and evidence dependencies where they can change the result. Two claims derived from the same unqualified assumption do not independently support the combination.

For a sequence, check whether the earlier transition can establish the next step's starting conditions within the declared horizon. A planned learning result remains a condition on the later step until learning evidence for the intended holder supports it. A later resource estimate must include continuing demands inherited from earlier steps. For alternatives, preserve their separate bases: a conflict between them may rule out simultaneous realization without ruling out either alone.

Use the domain analysis when the joint question needs scheduling, engineering, safety, organizational allocation, finance, or another specialized result. An ordinary arithmetic conflict can be stated when the underlying quantities and limits are qualified. Do not infer independence, additivity, or a probability of completion from the shape of a dependency diagram.

#### C.40.JF:4.4 - Include transition burden, recovery, and future options

Check what must happen to move from the starting configuration to the candidate, not just what could hold after arrival. Include material setup, learning, migration, evaluation, coordination, temporary loss of service, and continuing support. Distinguish the resources needed to find out whether a transition is possible from those needed to realize it.

When comparing the finite change's contribution, use [C.11.CRC](https://github.com/ailev/FPF/blob/main/FPF-Spec.md): actual or relied-on starting configuration, finite change, candidate configuration, common horizon and scenarios, and the complete relevant result, resource, affected-System/distributional, and protected coordinates. Each coordinate keeps its declared scale and polarity. A gain in the intended result does not cancel a protected loss or establish Pareto improvement.

Name what reversal would require and what it would not restore. Removing a tool may restore the earlier operating arrangement while leaving setup time spent, information disclosed, or a supplier relationship changed. State whether the transition opens, preserves, closes, or delays a later option, and which part of that claim is evidenced or conditional.

A proposed probe is itself a finite change when it consumes resources, exposes people or data, or changes later possibilities. Return its question and missing feasibility or authority result before treating it as an admissible recommendation or performed Work.

#### C.40.JF:4.5 - Return the strongest supported reachability claim

Write the result at the smallest affected scope.

| What the qualification establishes | Useful return |
| --- | --- |
| A compatible route or an applicable existence argument supports the transitions and their joint limits for this subject, configuration, use, and horizon | State bounded reachability, the supporting conditions, residual uncertainty, and the event that would reopen it. |
| A material premise is missing but the candidate is specified well enough to qualify | Retain a conditional opportunity; name the claim withheld, supplying practice, and result that could support or defeat it. |
| A qualified constraint defeats the proposed transition or combination | Reject that route for the stated use, or construct a revised candidate; retain independently supported alternatives. |
| The starting condition, finite change, or jointly necessary conditions are too unclear to test | Return an unresolved candidate and the missing description or evidence, without asserting reachability or impossibility. |

“Conditional” is useful only when the condition changes the result. State what a positive and a negative answer would do. Do not retain a known-infeasible direction as merely awaiting more evidence, or call an unanswered question a negative result.

A short answer may suffice.

> The two departments each need two hours of the same engineer's time every week. For this inquiry the engineer must review each department's confidential material separately. Two hours a week are available. Either review fits. Together they need four hours, so they cannot both be delivered in the eight weeks while urgent operating cover is protected.

This return need not become a large dependency register. Expand it when several recipients, delayed reuse, disagreement, or consequence makes the basis hard to recover.

**Recognition and assurance.** A reader can recognize a usable account from the named transition, joint limits, qualified or missing premises, and bounded return. Trust in the reachability claim requires the domain evidence and uncertainty assessment; high-consequence use retains its additional safety, authority, and assurance requirements. A clear account makes those requirements visible without satisfying them by description.

#### C.40.JF:4.6 - Return to the next use

Supply the qualified direction or gap to the current inquiry. C.40.OP may retain the candidate or reconstruct its path; the applicable professional engagement Method bounds an advising engagement, and decision-support Methods supply their results when recommendation is requested. Human-capability, organization, AI, engineering, operations, and other practices retain programme construction, allocation, realization, and evidence.

Reopen only the affected claim when its subject, starting configuration, use, horizon, resource, support, permission, protection, or evidence changes. Use C.40.RQ to trace that change through dependent opportunities and advice. Preserve an unchanged independent result.
